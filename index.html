Policy and terms

Before you book

Appointment confirmation

Booking requests are reviewed by Vita-Drip Lounge before final confirmation. You may be contacted to confirm availability, service suitability, and any preparation notes.

IV therapy screening

IV therapy and injectable-style services may require a wellness screening. Please disclose allergies, medications, pregnancy, or existing medical conditions when booking.

Cancellation window

Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your visit. Late arrivals may shorten the appointment or require rescheduling.
